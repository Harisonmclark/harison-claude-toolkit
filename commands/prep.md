Prepare me for this interview: $ARGUMENTS

1. Reorient on memory: /profile.md, /preferences.md, /areas/job-search.md and the /people/ file for anyone named.
2. Check Gmail and Google Calendar for the interview thread and event (time, format, who calls whom). Read only.
3. Research the interviewer and team from public sources (bio, recent publications or projects, the team's current priorities). Only include what you actually retrieved, with links.
4. Give me:
   - A 30-second "tell me about yourself" in my voice, true to my real background.
   - 6 to 8 likely questions for this role, each with 2 or 3 bullet points drawn from my real experience (no invented numbers or roles).
   - 3 smart questions to ask them, tied to their current work.
   - Any gap they may probe (years of experience, credentials) with an honest way to address it.
   - A short thank-you email draft to send within 24 hours.
5. Keep it to one screen plus the drafts. No em dashes. Never overstate my experience.
